Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Firbot collects, uses, shares and protects information when a business connects its WhatsApp Business Account to Firbot and uses Firbot's automation services.

1. Who we are

Firbot provides business process automation and WhatsApp automation services to businesses. Through its integration with Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API), Firbot builds and operates automations that a business customer configures and explicitly authorizes.

2. Information we process

Depending on the automations a business configures, Firbot may process:

  • WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) identifiers;
  • WhatsApp phone number identifiers;
  • Business profile information associated with the connected account;
  • WhatsApp messages sent to or from the connected business, which may include the names, phone numbers and message content of the people the business communicates with;
  • Message metadata, such as timestamps, delivery and read statuses, and message types;
  • Webhook events sent by Meta for the connected account;
  • Other information necessary to operate the automations the business has configured, and contact details the business provides to Firbot.

3. How we obtain information

  • From the business, via Meta. When a business completes Meta's official signup flow and authorizes Firbot, Meta provides Firbot with the identifiers and permissions needed to access the business's WhatsApp Business assets.
  • From Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. Once connected, Meta delivers messages, statuses and other events for the connected account to Firbot through webhooks and APIs.
  • Directly from the business. For example, when the business contacts Firbot or provides information to configure its automations.

4. How we use information

Firbot uses this information only to:

  • provide the automation services the business has explicitly requested;
  • maintain and operate those services, including sending and receiving messages on the business's behalf;
  • troubleshoot errors and provide support;
  • improve the reliability and quality of the services provided to that business;
  • comply with legal obligations and enforce applicable terms.

Firbot does not use this information for advertising, and does not sell it.

5. WhatsApp Business Platform and Meta integration

Firbot connects to WhatsApp through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. A business authorizes the connection itself through Meta's official flow, and only the assets the business chooses to share are made available to Firbot. Firbot does not receive the business's Facebook or Meta password.

Firbot's use of information received from Meta APIs is subject to the applicable Meta Platform Terms, Meta's developer policies and the WhatsApp Business Platform requirements and policies. Meta's own processing of information is governed by Meta's privacy policies. Firbot is an independent service provider and is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, Meta, Facebook or WhatsApp.

6. Third-party service providers (processors)

Firbot uses third-party infrastructure and service providers to operate its services. These providers process information on Firbot's behalf and only to deliver the services. They include:

  • Meta, as the operator of the WhatsApp Business Platform;
  • Make.com, to run automation workflows;
  • OpenAI, only for automations where AI functionality is explicitly part of what the business has configured, in which case relevant message content may be sent to OpenAI's services for processing;
  • hosting, storage, email and similar infrastructure providers.

Sharing information with service providers so they can operate the services on our behalf is not a sale of personal information.

7. Data sharing

Firbot does not sell personal information. Firbot only shares information:

  • with the service providers described above, to operate the services;
  • with the business that owns the connected account, and with systems that business has instructed Firbot to connect;
  • when required by law, or to protect the rights, safety or security of Firbot, its customers or others;
  • with the business's consent or at its direction.

8. Data retention

Firbot keeps information only as long as needed to provide the services the business has requested, troubleshoot them, meet legal obligations and resolve disputes. Service providers such as Make.com may keep execution logs according to their own retention settings. When information is no longer needed, or after a valid deletion request, Firbot deletes it or makes it anonymous within a reasonable period, unless it must be kept for legal reasons.

9. Data security

Firbot uses reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information. These include encrypted (HTTPS) connections, restricted access to credentials and systems, and keeping secret credentials out of public code. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so Firbot cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. International processing and transfers

Firbot and its service providers, including Meta, Make.com and OpenAI, may process information in countries other than the one where the business or its contacts are located. Those countries may have different data protection laws. Where applicable law requires it, Firbot relies on the safeguards made available by its service providers for such transfers.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you are located, you may have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your information, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. If you are a person who has exchanged messages with a business that uses Firbot, that business decides how your information is used. You may contact the business directly, or contact Firbot and we will help route your request.

To request access to or correction of information, email leo@firbotacademy.com.

12. Data Deletion Requests

Users and businesses may request deletion of data associated with their WhatsApp integration by contacting Firbot.

To request deletion of data associated with your WhatsApp Business integration, contact leo@firbotacademy.com with the subject "Data Deletion Request". Please identify the business or WhatsApp number associated with the request. Firbot may request reasonable verification before processing the request.

Firbot will confirm once the request has been processed. Some information may be kept where the law requires it.

13. Disconnecting your WhatsApp Business Account

A business can stop Firbot's access to its WhatsApp Business Account at any time by:

  • removing Firbot's access from its business settings in Meta Business Suite / Meta Business Manager; or
  • emailing leo@firbotacademy.com and asking Firbot to disconnect the account.

After disconnection, Firbot will no longer receive new messages or events for that account. To also have existing data deleted, submit a Data Deletion Request as described above.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

Firbot may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date. If changes are significant, Firbot will take reasonable steps to notify connected businesses.

15. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy or how Firbot handles information, contact: leo@firbotacademy.com.
